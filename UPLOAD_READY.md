## 💡 **TIPS**

### **Best Image Practices:**
- **Size:** 800×800px to 1000×1000px recommended
- **Format:** JPG for photos, PNG for graphics
- **File size:** Under 2MB for faster processing
- **Quality:** Use clear, well-lit product photos

### **For Organic Powders:**
- Image 1: Package front (primary)
- Image 2: Package back/ingredients
- Image 3: Product close-up
- Image 4: In use/benefits
